Featured research

What RL post-training actually changes

RLVR improves accuracy, and the claims about it go further. My work looks inside the update and at the reasoning it leaves behind.

Ongoing work

GRPO-Unhinged: The Hidden Advantage of Classification in RL Post-Training

With binary rewards the clipped GRPO update is a weighted hinge loss on outcome-labeled tokens. A per-class loss normalization raised plan accuracy by 10% over GRPO on 30×30 mazes and kept incorrect responses from doubling in length.

Read the project → Under review · ICLR 2027

RLVR as Prompt Augmentation Reinforces Both Correct and Incorrect Reasoning

The optimal RLVR policy is the base policy conditioned on success, so a chain of thought is up-weighted by how much it raises the solve probability, valid or not.

Read the paper page → NeurIPS 2025 · LAW workshop

RL in Name Only?

Under the LLM-as-MDP assumptions used in RLVR, on-policy GRPO reduces to filtered iterative SFT, and the growth in response length traces back to these assumptions.

Read the paper page → NeurIPS 2025 · MATH-AI workshop

Local Coherence or Global Validity?

A first-order-logic measure of trace coherence shows that RLVR improves local step coherence without making solutions valid.

How I work

A critical outlook and skeptical evaluation

I take a critical look at RL post-training and enjoy evaluation work that tests LLMs to their limits.

A critical look at RL post-training

What RL with verifiable rewards changes in a model, and what it only appears to change. Theory of the update, checked with post-training runs in verl.

Skeptical evaluation of reasoning

Measuring the reasoning, not only the final answer. Trace coherence, trace validity, and correct answers reached through incoherent reasoning.

Multi-turn and agentic RL

Credit assignment across turns as well as tokens, and evaluation of reasoning in non-ergodic worlds, where one bad outcome can end a trajectory.
